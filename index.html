Frequently Asked Questions

What can I buy from the BI Solutions Digital Store?

Our store offers ready-to-use business templates, spreadsheets, dashboards, and other management tools built for everyday operations. Each product page lists what is included, the platform it runs on (for example Google Sheets, Excel, or Power BI), and any limitations, so you know exactly what you are getting before you order.

How do I order and pay?

No account is needed. Choose a product, enter your name, email, and mobile number, and you will receive an order reference number with the payment details. You can pay by GCash or bank transfer, then enter your payment reference number on the same screen so we can match it to your order.

How will I receive my purchase?

Once we have verified your payment in our account, we email your access or download link to the address you provided. Please double-check your email address and look in your spam folder. If nothing arrives, contact us with your order reference number and we will check your order and resend the link.

What software do I need, and can I use my own data?

It depends on the product, and each product page states the platform and requirements. A free Google account is enough for our Google Sheets products. The tools are designed so you can enter or paste your own figures, and each one comes with instructions to help you get started.

What consulting services does BI Solutions offer, and who do you work with?

We offer regulatory and compliance support (ISO consultation, FDA LTO and CPR, and government permitting), business consulting and analysis (process mapping, KPIs, and business health checks), and data and management tools such as custom dashboards and trackers. We work with individuals, SMEs, growing organizations, and established corporations.

Can I get a refund?

Because digital products cannot be returned once delivered, refunds are reviewed case by case. If something is wrong with your purchase, such as a file that will not open or the wrong product, contact us with your order reference number and we will work with you to fix it or find a fair resolution.

Can you customize a dashboard or tracker for my business, and how do I get a quotation?

Yes. We build custom dashboards and trackers in Power BI, Excel, Google Sheets, and Looker Studio around your own processes and data. Contact us with a short description of what you need, and we will review your requirements and discuss the scope, timeline, and pricing, which depends on the work involved.

Do you issue ISO certificates or approve permits?

No. ISO certificates are issued by accredited certification bodies, and permits, licenses, and registrations are issued by the relevant government agencies. We prepare your documentation, guide you through the process, and coordinate your requirements, but we cannot guarantee any approval or result.
